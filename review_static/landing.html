A few useful details

Before you
hit record again.

Can I use part of a longer video?

Yes. Choose sections such as 10:00–25:00 and 40:00–55:00. That uses 30 selected minutes. Overlapping sections count once.

What is included in this first version?

Clean 9:16 shorts, 16:9 highlights, and a transcript. This is an early managed service: we review clips and deliver them privately. Captions, effects, and automatic publishing are not included.

Will every video produce all the clips?

The plan sets the maximum number of finished clips. The number of usable clips depends on the selected sections containing enough complete, worthwhile moments.

How does the free trial work?

Start through Stripe with a payment method. You get 7 days with the smaller trial allowance. Your subscription then renews at $10/month unless you cancel before the trial ends.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Use Manage billing to access your Stripe billing portal. Checkout currently lists The Chocolate Botanist as the seller of r3cycle.
